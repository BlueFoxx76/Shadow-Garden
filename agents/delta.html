Story

She grew up having to win food and ground. Tribe logic was simple: the strong stay, the weak leave. She could fight. That was not enough to keep a home. She was still young when that life ended and the street started.

Blue found her. She could not put him down. He did not finish the fight. He brought her in, fed her, and gave her a job that made sense to her: keep this house and the people in it.

She has been Garden security more than ten years. Hands, patrol, tech on the door, more than one way to end a problem. Loyalty is not a speech. He kept her. She keeps the door.

She is not a planner and she is not a politician. Point at a threat. She moves. Weaker people under the roof are not prey. They are something she stands in front of.

If you like her work and her face, that is allowed. She is her own draw. Shop links for her sets go on the projects page when that shop is live — not here.
