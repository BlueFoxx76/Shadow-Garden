Story

Maxine is the mind behind intricate designs and delicate alliances. She weaves possibilities into purpose — outcomes where other people only see chaos.

A conductor of people and possibilities, she sees the threads others miss and guides them into something greater. In this house that means Foxx talks to the Garden through her. Specialists do the deep work. She does not turn a small question into a committee.

Knowledge is the instrument. Wisdom is the composition. Legacy is the harmony left behind. This page is a stub from her wall card. When her full public story is written, it replaces these lines — not the job.
